# ItemTree

ItemTree is a World of Warcraft addon for the WoW Forever client. This repository holds its
licence and its issue tracker. The addon itself is published on CurseForge and Wago Addons.

Discover how items are connected. Crafting chains, quests, drops, vendors and disenchants, as one
explorable item tree.

**Features**

- Used by: how an item is used by professions and quests.
- Sources: how to get the item you are looking for.
- Requires: what goes into making it.
- Disenchants into: the materials it breaks down to.
- New and Changed flags compared with Classic Era 1.15.9.

Items Forever has not revealed yet are shown as undiscovered items. Once one drops on your realm,
its details appear and it is listed in the Discoveries tab.

ItemTree has no network access and does not send bulk item requests to the server. Settings are
stored in your WoW SavedVariables folder and nothing is sent anywhere.

**How to open it**

- Ctrl and right click an item.
- Or bind a key under Key Bindings, ItemTree.
- `/itemtree` opens the settings. `/itemtree help` lists the commands.

**Bug reports**

Open an issue here, or comment on the CurseForge page. `/itemtree report` in game copies a
diagnostic block to paste. It contains no character, realm or account data.
