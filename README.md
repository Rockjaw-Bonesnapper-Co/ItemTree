# ItemTree

This repository holds the licence and the issue tracker for ItemTree, a World of Warcraft addon for WoW Forever. The addon itself is published on CurseForge and Wago Addons.

Discover how items are connected. Crafting chains, quests, drops, vendors and disenchants, as one explorable item tree.

Look through your inventory or search for any item in the game and understand how it relates to the professions and quests you care about.

Built for WoW Forever.

**Features**

- Used by: understand how items can be used by professions and quests.
- Sources: how to get the item you're looking for.
- Requires: what goes into making it.
- Disenchants into: the materials it breaks down to.
- New and Changed flags compared with Classic Era 1.15.9.

Bonus: items Forever has not revealed yet are shown as mystery items with their secrets yet to be discovered! Once one drops on your realm, its details appear and it is listed in the Discoveries tab.

**How to open it**

- Ctrl and right click an item.
- Or bind a key under Key Bindings, ItemTree.
- `/itemtree` opens the settings. `/itemtree help` lists the commands.

**Beta limits**

- Drop rates and sources are Classic Era data, marked Predicted. Forever has changed loot, so treat them as a guide. Untick "Show predicted data" to hide them.
- The beta client may not load saved variables, so settings can reset at login. This is a client bug that affects every addon.
- English only.

**Feedback**

- Open an issue here, or comment on the CurseForge page.
- `/itemtree report` in game copies a diagnostic block to paste. It contains no character, realm or account data.
